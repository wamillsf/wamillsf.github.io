Hey there detectives,

We are proud to announce that the game is out today.

Demo is also available today. It covers the first beat of the story and should give a pretty good idea on what to expect from the rest of the game.

This is the first of the Sammy Jukes series. We are going to continue the series with new cases and improve upon each entry with your feedback. Please don't hesitate to share your thoughts on the game in the discussion pages and elsewhere.

We are currently working on a few quality of life updates right now, and will be for the coming few months.

We hope you guys enjoy the game. We sure did enjoy making it!

Also we'd like to thank everyone who helped us during the development. Especially all our voice actors and the pre-launch players who shared their very valuable feedback.

Good hunting!
